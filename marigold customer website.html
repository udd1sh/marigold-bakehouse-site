FAQ

How can I place an order?

Message Marigold Bakehouse through WhatsApp or Instagram.

What products are currently listed?

Sourdough Loaf, Chocolate Babka, Almond Croissant and Cardamom Bun.

Can I order multiple quantities?

Yes. Tell us the quantity you want for each product.

What happens after I send my order?

The order details are collected through the messaging workflow and entered into the bakery's seller order system.

Where can I ask about something that isn't listed?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or Instagram and the ordering assistant can help with the enquiry.
